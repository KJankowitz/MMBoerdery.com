Who We Are

MengelMoes Boerdery Pty Ltd is a sustainable agricultural enterprise specializing in commercial meat rabbit production and moringa cultivation. Our focus is on producing high-quality protein and agricultural products using efficient and environmentally responsible farming systems.

Our Mission

To produce sustainable, high-quality agricultural products while maintaining excellent animal welfare and environmental standards.

Our Vision

To become a leading supplier of premium meat rabbits and moringa products in Southern Africa.
