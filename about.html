Our story

MengelMoes Boerdery Pty Ltd is a sustainable agricultural enterprise specializing in commercial meat rabbit production and moringa cultivation.

We are a family-driven agricultural business focused on growing healthy food, supporting livestock systems, and building resilient solutions for modern farming.

Our Mission

To produce sustainable, high-quality agricultural products while maintaining excellent animal welfare and environmental standards.

Our Vision

To become a leading supplier of premium meat rabbits and moringa products in Southern Africa.

What Guides Us

Integrity, quality, resilience, and a practical commitment to sustainable growth across every stage of the farming process.
